Privacy Policy

Updated: September 7, 2026

Wenshu Travel respects your privacy. This page explains how information submitted through consultation and product experience request forms is used and protected.

Information we collect

When you submit a form, we may collect your phone or WhatsApp number, name, company, company address, business type, and the requirements you choose to share.

How we use it

We use this information only to discuss inbound-tourism growth solutions, arrange product experiences, answer inquiries, and improve our consultation workflow.

Consultation storage

Submitted forms and messages sent to human support are stored in the xuntingtravel system for follow-up by authorized staff. They are not displayed publicly. A temporary visitor-session identifier is stored in this browser tab to retrieve your support replies.

How we protect it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We take reasonable steps to limit access to people who need it for solution consultation.

Your choices

To request correction or deletion of submitted information, contact a Wenshu Travel advisor through the hotline shown on the site.
